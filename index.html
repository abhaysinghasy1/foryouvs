For Your Eyes Only

Some stories are not meant to be opened by everyone.
This one is only for you.

Chapter 01 · The Beginning

I Had No Idea
That Day Would
Become So Special…

It was the time of our 10th board exams. Classes weren't going on, but we still used to go to coaching. I studied at Mukesh Sir's coaching, while you studied at V1.

That day, I was standing at the crossroads with Diwakar, Vivek and Aryaveer.

You, Divya and Smariti were coming from the side of Sanskriti School.

And that day, something happened that you probably don't even remember.

You weren't wearing a mask.

And maybe that was the first time I properly saw your face.

You were wearing a dark green top.

Maybe you don't remember what you were wearing that day. Maybe you don't even remember that particular moment.

But I do.

Because for me, that wasn't just another normal day.

That was the day I started having feelings for you.

“I saw a face among the crowd that day,
and somehow, my heart chose to stay.
I didn't know what I was feeling then,
I just knew I wanted to see that face again.”

Chapter 02 · The First Step

And Then
I Took The
First Step…

After that day, I kept thinking about you.

So eventually, I was the one who messaged you first.

Maybe even I didn't know where that one message was going to take me.

We started talking.

I think it had only been three or four days when I decided to tell you what I actually felt.

I confessed my feelings.

But your answer wasn't what my heart had hoped for.

You said no.

You told me that you weren't interested in these things.

You even said a few more things to me at that time.

And after that…

We stopped talking.

“I told you what my heart wanted to say,
but your answer pushed me away.
I thought that was the end of our story,
but my heart wasn't ready to let you go.”

Maybe I thought the story had ended there.

But I didn't know… that it was only the beginning.

Chapter 03 · A Little Secret

Then Came
The Dimension
Chapter…

A few days later, I messaged you again.

That's when I found out that you were thinking about doing non-schooling from Dimension.

You told me that your family wanted you to go to ABC.

Maybe Bhaiya had also suggested that you do non-schooling.

And somewhere, you also wanted to come to ABC.

But eventually, you joined Dimension.

Then something happened there.

You had a fight with a girl at Dimension.

Anand told me about it.

So I messaged you around that time.

You didn't really want to tell me what had happened. But I already knew.

I told you everything that had happened at the coaching.

You were surprised.

“How do you know all this?”

Not once.

You asked me almost four or five times.

But I never told you that Anand had told me.

And that was the first time…

I lied to you.

“Sometimes a lie isn't born from a bad intention,
sometimes it's born from the fear of losing someone.”

Chapter 04 · Navratri

Then Came
Navratri…

After some time, something happened that still feels like more than just a coincidence.

It was Navratri.

And somehow, we met again.

You were there.

I was there too.

Diwakar and Vivek were literally trying to stop me and saying—

“Bro, go meet her.”

But I didn't.

Because one thought was going through my mind—

“Maybe she won't like it if I go.”

So I walked away.

Maybe it sounds like a very small thing now. But at that moment, I thought respecting your comfort was more important than meeting you.

After some time, you guys came there too.

And later, Diwakar and Vivek gave me an earful for leaving like that.

Then we all went away.

“You were right there, but I still walked away,
not because I didn't want to meet you,
but because I was scared you wouldn't want me to stay.”

But once again… the story didn't end.

Chapter 05 · The Same Story

And Then
The Same Story
Started Repeating…

After that, we would talk once in a while.

Sometimes I would message you after a few months.

We would talk for two or three days. Sometimes maybe four or five days at most.

And then…

You would delete Snapchat.

Or you would say a few things to me and tell me—

“Don't message me again.”

“Every time you said, ‘Don't message me again,’
I told myself, ‘Okay, this time I won't.’”

And me?

I would stay quiet.

For a few days. Sometimes weeks. Sometimes months.

And then somehow…

I would come back and message you again.

Maybe from your point of view, it looked like I just didn't understand what you were saying.

But honestly, even I couldn't understand why I kept coming back.

Maybe because it's easy to tell your mind to move on. But it's not always that easy to convince your heart.

You kept creating distance… and I kept finding my way back to you.

Chapter 06 · Another Coincidence

Then You
Started College…

Time kept moving.

Then your college started.

We talked around that time too.

You told me about your hostel.

We talked about your college life.

But somehow, even then, our conversations didn't last very long.

And once again…

We stopped talking.

And then something happened again.

Another coincidence.

Ranidiha Chauraha

We met at Ranidiha Chauraha.

After all that time.

After all those conversations.

After all those moments of getting close and then becoming strangers again.

Another crossroads.

And there we were… once again, somewhere in the same story.

“The road brought us back to where we least expected,
after all the distance, after all the things left unsaid.”

Chapter 07 · To Be Continued

Maybe This
Wasn't The End…

Maybe our story has always had something with crossroads.

Because the story that started at one crossroads… somehow brought us back to another one.

We got close. We became distant. We stopped talking. We talked again. We walked away. And somehow, life kept putting us in the same story.

“Maybe it wasn't just a coincidence,
maybe our story simply wasn't finished yet.”

And maybe…

that wasn't the end of our story either.

Maybe it was simply the beginning of the next chapter.

And this time, I don't want to write the next chapter alone.
